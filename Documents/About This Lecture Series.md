# About This Lecture Series

**Lectures on Faith is a real-world lecture series** written by Penelope, in active and ongoing use: faith affirmation lectures, contextualized comparatively, "in context with other words in other lectures on other faiths." Not a coding project; it is creative and theological writing that uses Intricate as its spatial canvas.

**The workflow runs through the family's own instruments.** Theological and philosophical points are discussed in conversation (including with Claude), notes are taken on both her own phrasing and Claude's input, and the writing then moves to The Majestic for composition and presentation. The Intricate session is the spatial thinking surface; this repo holds the series itself, its pages, images, clips, and the InDesign master.

**Provenance of this note.** These paragraphs lived in the Claude memory corpus from 2026-06-06 and were offloaded here on 2026-09-01, per Penelope's 2026-07-30 direction that notes about the lectures belong with the lectures. What remains in memory is the one standing rule that governs sessions rather than this project: work may blend, codebase and theology in the same breath, both real.
